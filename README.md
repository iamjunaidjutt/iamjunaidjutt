I build backend systems and web applications, with a focus on practical AI.

- 💼 **Current role**: Associate Software Engineer (AI/ML) at **Devsinc** in Lahore. I work on **LawPractice.ai**, an AI platform used by more than 300 plaintiff law firms in the United States.
- ⚙️ **What I do**: Most of my work is backend engineering with **ASP.NET Core, LLMs, and RAG**. We take messy case files and medical records that can be over 1,000 pages long and turn them into structured documents like demand letters. That work helped cut preparation time by about **70%**, made turnaround about **7x faster**, and reduced documentation errors by about **90%**. I also help maintain our OCR and document pipelines and resolve production issues.

Over time, I moved into full-stack and backend systems—leading a team of three on a real-time CRM with live rider tracking, and building my own projects with WebSockets, Prisma, and Supabase.
